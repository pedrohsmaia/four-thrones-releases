## Welcome

Tripothon S1 is "The First World-Building 3D Hackathon": world-building with AI x 3D,
open to developers, artists, students and indie makers worldwide. Official website:
https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en/events/tripothon-s1. Discord:
https://discord.com/invite/NEdkyQQ3VP. Contact (site): tripothon01@vastai3d.com.

## 1. Event overview

### 1.1 Theme: "A Gift for ______"

"Build a world as a gift: for the kid I used to be, for future me, for the aliens about
to arrive, for the stray cat on the corner, for one having a hard time, for my BFF who
is 8 time zones away, for the moon's dark side... Your world can be big or small: a
breathing city, a memory you can walk into, a planet that existed for only six hours.
The only standard is: when the world is unveiled, someone would want to stay and
explore it."

### 1.2 Key timeline

| Phase | Date | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Registration opens | From Sep 1 | Register on the official website, select your track and region |
| Kickoff online session | Sep 13 | Jury members and partners share toolkits, tutorials, creative guidance |
| Online submission period | Sep 15 to Oct 5 | Submit across 5 tracks for the global online prize pool |
| Offline Demo Days | Oct 5 to Oct 20 | Live demos and judging in 7 cities; attendees also compete for regional pools |
| Jury review period | Oct 5 to Oct 25 | Online and offline judging run simultaneously |
| Winner announcement | Oct 25 | Global online winners announced |

**Hard deadline: online submissions close on October 5. Late submissions will not be
accepted.** The submission portal states it exactly: **October 5, 23:59 AoE (UTC-12)**,
which is October 6, 11:59 UTC (08:59 in Brasilia).

## 2. Tracks and team formation

### 2.1 Direction tracks (required, choose one)

Tool-agnostic: use whatever 3D or world-building software you like.

| # | Track | Description | Accepted formats |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Games | Story-driven / interactive puzzle / emotional experiences | Video, App, Web, Standalone |
| 02 | Film / VFX | Short films, animation, VFX, 3D previs workflow | App, Web, Standalone |
| 03 | VR / XR / AR | VR experiences, mobile AR filters, WebAR, spatial and immersive experiences | App, Web, Mini-program |
| 04 | Apps and AI-native tools | Web apps, AI-native products, creative tools, interactive apps, mini-programs | Web, App, Standalone |
| 05 | Physical design | 3D printing, product design, installations, sculptures, sandboxes | Physical objects, model files, 3D-printed work |

The site words the Games track as "Playable worlds, mechanics, narrative systems,
AI-assisted game development".

### 2.2 Tool tracks (optional, choose up to three)

Presented by Tripo's partners. Selecting a tool track makes you eligible for that
tool's prize, **but the project must actually use the tool, or it is disqualified from
that tool track.** Partners: Tripo, PICO, Heygears, World Labs (the site also lists
Jupiter SR). "The same project can win both a direction track award and multiple tool
track awards; prizes are stackable."

### 2.3 Team rules

- Solo entries are welcome, as are teams of 2 to 3 people.
- Cross-disciplinary mixes are encouraged: tech (full-stack / AI) + creative (designers / artists).
- Each team designates a team lead at registration, who submits the final work on behalf of the team.
- Teammates: the official Discord's #looking-for-team channel.

## 3. Submission requirements

### 3.1 Deliverables by track

All tracks may additionally submit a Public Build Log for bonus scoring consideration.

**Games / VR / XR / AR / Apps track**

| # | Deliverable | Required? | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Complete playable demo | Required | A complete demo that judges can play and try. Formats: Web / H5 / App / executable, etc. |
| 2 | Demo walkthrough video | Required | A 1 to 2 minute walkthrough that takes the user inside your world (not a trailer cut). Must include core feature demos; voiceover is recommended. |
| 3 | Visual asset board | Required | 3+ high-res screenshots / GIFs of key scenes, including key stills, multi-view turnarounds, and environment frames. |

**Film / VFX track**: final film (required), project files (required), film story
statement (required). **Physical design track**: complete physical work showcase,
multi-view photography, operation demo video if applicable, in-context usage photos.

**Optional (all tracks): Public build log.** Public development progress posts on
social media during the hackathon. The most active builders get amplified by the
official team and can earn bonus scoring consideration.

### 3.2 How to submit

1. Register on the official website and log in to the participant center.
2. Select your direction track (required) and tool tracks (optional).
3. Upload all deliverables during the submission window (Sep 15 to Oct 5).
4. You can revise and update your submission before the deadline; the final version is judged.

Two links on the official site: "Start Building" opens the participation Typeform
(https://tripo-ambassador.typeform.com/to/ScfyutRF?typeform-lang=en, the registration);
"Submit / View Project" opens the submission portal **https://activity.tripo3d.ai/en/submit**,
which needs a sign-in with the Tripo account (the one used on the Tripo API platform) and
has five steps: Team (name, 1 to 3 members), Project (name, tagline, description, tracks),
Media and Demo (cover, stills or photos, video, the demo or project files the track
requires), Awards and Declarations (tool contributions, third-party disclosure, prior-work
declaration, rights), Review. Progress can be saved and resumed before the deadline.

## 4. Judging and prizes

### 4.1 Direction track judging criteria

| Criterion | Weight | What judges look for |
|---|---|---|
| Creativity | 30% | Is the idea original and unique? How is it different from existing solutions? |
| Completeness | 25% | Is it built? Can you play / watch / use it? Is the experience polished? |
| Theme alignment | 20% | Who is the gift for? How moving is it? How well does it align with "A Gift for ______"? |
| Viral potential | 15% | Does the project have breakout potential? Can it spark discussion and sharing? |
| Commercial value | 10% | What's the long-term potential? Is there room for commercialization or continued iteration? |

### 4.2 Tool track judging criteria

| Criterion | Weight | What judges look for |
|---|---|---|
| Inventive use of the tool | 35% | New ways to use the tool; beyond conventional usage; pushing the tool's original boundaries |
| Tool synergy | 25% | Multiple tools combined into a complete workflow; smooth, efficient handoffs |
| Tool contribution | 20% | How central is the tool: the core driving force or an auxiliary aid? |
| Theme fit | 10% | Alignment with "A Gift for ______": who is it for, how moving is it? |
| Breakout potential | 10% | Reach; can the approach to the tool spark industry discussion and sharing? |

### 4.3 Global online prize pool

Handbook ($13,200 total): Global Top 1 $3,000, Top 2 $2,000, Top 3 $1,000; Best Game
Project $800; Best XR / VR Project $800; Best AI Filmmaking Project $800; Best
Application Project $800; Best Physical Design Project $800; Best Use of Tripo / PICO /
Heygears / World Labs $800 each (tool track awards). All submitted projects, whether or
not they attend an offline Demo Day, are eligible.

The official site (read the same day) shows larger numbers: Top 1 $5,000, Top 2 $4,000,
Top 3 $3,000, the same $800 track and tool awards, and "$80,000 total prize pool"
across all pools. The site is the newer statement.

### 4.4 Regional offline prize pool

Participants who attend an offline Demo Day compete for the global online pool and
their region's offline pool (awarded on site). US region (San Francisco): Top 1 $3,000,
Top 2 $2,000, Top 3 $1,000 (site: $5,000 / $3,500 / $2,200); tool awards $800 each plus
2 Logitech gaming mouse bundles and 2 Xbox consoles. Other regions: Top 1 $2,500, Top 2
$1,500, Top 3 $1,200; tool awards $400 each plus the same bundles.

### 4.5 Social media prize

Most Viewed Post, Most Liked Post, Most Quoted Post on X: one Mac mini each, counted as
of October 20 across Instagram, Facebook, X, YouTube and TikTok; counts must be publicly
visible. Post about the Tripothon S1 journey with the hashtag **#Tripothon** and tag
**@tripoai**. The site adds: at least 1,000 likes to be eligible.

## 7. Guidelines and notes

### 7.1 Code of conduct

- **Respect originality.** Do not reproduce or use others' work without permission.
  Open-source models, front-end scaffolds (Cursor, v0, Next.js) and generative AI tools
  are allowed, "but core business logic and asset integration must be completed during
  the competition period."
- Express responsibly; reject discrimination; communicate kindly.

### 7.2 On secondary development

"Using open-source models, front-end scaffolds (like Cursor, v0, Next.js), and various
generative AI tools is allowed. If you had an idea or a simple attempt before the
competition and built a presentable work based on it during the hackathon, that does
not count as secondary development.

If, before the hackathon began and in any capacity, you completed the code,
development, or construction of the same project, it counts as secondary development.
**You must proactively declare the foundation and your new additions at submission
time. Failure to declare, or false declaration, will result in disqualification if
verified.**

Judges score only the 'work increment', i.e. only the new features and innovations
created during the competition period."

### 7.3 IP and copyright

- All intellectual property rights of submitted works belong to the original author or
  relevant rights holder.
- By participating, you grant Tripo AI a free, non-exclusive license to display,
  distribute and use the submitted work for purposes related to the event and Tripo AI
  platform promotion; Tripo AI makes reasonable efforts to retain author IDs, usernames
  and source attribution.
- For AIGC works, mind content sources and copyright ownership: do not use unauthorized
  media (images, videos, audio, lyrics...).
- Content that violates laws, exploits suffering or vulnerable groups, incites
  confrontation or violates public order is prohibited; violations mean removal and
  disqualification.

## 8. FAQ

- **Win without attending a Demo Day?** Yes; all works compete for the global online pool.
- **Only Blender / Unreal / other tools?** Yes; direction tracks are tool-agnostic. A
  tool-specific award requires actually using Tripo, PICO, Heygears or World Labs.
- **Solo or team?** Solo and teams of 2 to 3 are welcome.
- **Multiple tracks?** One direction track per project plus 0 to 3 tool tracks; a project
  can win a direction award and several tool awards.
- **Revise after uploading?** Yes, until the October 5 deadline; the final version is
  judged; after the deadline the portal closes.
- **Prize money?** After the October 25 announcement the team contacts winners; regional
  awards are presented on site.
- **Social media prize?** #Tripothon and @tripoai on Instagram, Facebook, X, YouTube or
  TikTok; most views, likes and quote posts on X as of October 20 win a Mac mini each.
